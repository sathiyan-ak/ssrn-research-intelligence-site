How this works

The rules behind every number on this site.

What is a niche?

A niche is a group of papers that share a topic or keyword. The site scores 3,241 niches and shows the top 40 candidates by score.

Verdicts

  • Growing: the last 90 days have more than 25% more papers than the 90 days before.
  • Falling: more than 25% fewer.
  • Flat: in between.
  • Too few papers to judge: under 30 papers in either window.

Which niches make the grid

A niche is in the grid when at least 50% of its papers have a primary topic in economics, finance or business subfields. "Primary" is the highest-scoring topic per paper, a stand-in and not OpenAlex's primary topic. The grid holds the first 12 such niches by score. Niches past that display cap, and niches that fail the rule, are listed below the grid with their counts.

Early signal

These are early signals from about one year of data. They have not been checked against later outcomes, so they are not predictions.

Where the papers come from

Papers come from OpenAlex and Crossref (SSRN records). A day with an upstream gap is marked on the data health page.
